## Services

### ReservationService

ReservationService turns holds into confirmed bookings and cancels them.  It is where HoldService's conversion is driven from: a hold is never converted by a caller reaching into HoldService, only by a reservation being made of it. There is no UpdateReservation -- the window and resources are the hold's, and changing either is a new hold and a new reservation.

| Method | Request | Response | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CreateReservation` | `CreateReservationRequest` | `Reservation` | Makes a reservation from a live hold, converting the hold so its resources stay taken.  Idempotent: the reservation's id is the hold's, so a retry returns the reservation the first call made. Fails with FAILED_PRECONDITION when the hold has lapsed or been released. |
| `GetReservation` | `GetReservationRequest` | `Reservation` | Retrieves one reservation. |
| `ListReservations` | `ListReservationsRequest` | `ListReservationsResponse` | Lists a site's reservations. |
| `CancelReservation` | `CancelReservationRequest` | `Reservation` | Cancels a reservation, freeing its resources.  Idempotent: cancelling a cancelled reservation succeeds and changes nothing. |

## Messages

### Reservation

A Reservation is a confirmed booking: a hold made permanent.  It is the last step of booking, not the first. The caller checks availability, takes a hold, settles whatever its vertical needs settled -- payment, a guest list, a waiver -- and then creates a reservation from the hold. The reservation records the outcome; the engine already holds the resources.  Deliberately thin. scheduling.v1's Booking carried price, discount, promo code, refund, guests and occupancy, which made it a hotel booking. None of that is here: money belongs to the payment service and people to the user service, both referenced by name (AIP-215), and anything else a vertical needs to remember rides in `attributes`.

## Enums

### ReservationState

Lifecycle of a Reservation.  Two of these states are in-between states, and they are why a crash never leaves the books wrong. Confirming takes two writes -- this record and the hold's conversion -- as does cancelling, and no transaction spans the engine and this table on every backend. So each transition is written down before it is carried out: a reservation seen PENDING or CANCELLING says what was being attempted, and the reconciler finishes it.

| Value | Number | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RESERVATION_STATE_UNSPECIFIED` | 0 | Default; never persisted. |
| `RESERVATION_STATE_PENDING` | 1 | Recorded, and its hold is being converted. Transient: the call that created it, a retry of that call, or the reconciler moves it on to CONFIRMED or FAILED. |
| `RESERVATION_STATE_CONFIRMED` | 2 | The hold was converted: the resources are taken for the window for good. |
| `RESERVATION_STATE_CANCELLING` | 3 | Cancellation is recorded and the resources are being freed. Transient, like PENDING, and finished the same way. |
| `RESERVATION_STATE_CANCELLED` | 4 | Cancelled; the resources are free again. Terminal. |
| `RESERVATION_STATE_FAILED` | 5 | The hold lapsed or was released before it could be converted, so nothing was taken. Terminal. |
